Track homeschooling against the Flemish minimum goals

Doelenboek is a free web app for parents who homeschool in Flanders, Belgium. Note down what you do with your children, with photos, link it to the Flemish minimum goals (minimumdoelen) and see per child and per subject which goals have been covered and which are still open.

Open the app

How it works

  1. Add your children

    Each child gets a name and a small animal picture, so you recognise them at a glance on every screen.

  2. Note down what you do

    A walk in the woods, cooking together, a picture book: write it down briefly, pick the date and the children who took part, and add photos.

  3. Link it to the minimum goals

    For each activity, pick the minimum goals you worked on. Doelenboek includes the Flemish minimum goals by subject and category, with their official code.

  4. See what is still open

    Per child and per subject you see how many goals have been covered, how often, and which ones not yet. Mark when a child has mastered a goal.

What you get

  • Free, without ads and without cookies on your device
  • In Dutch and English; the goals are available in both languages
  • Works in the browser on any computer, tablet or phone
  • An account with email and password, so your data is on all your devices
  • Photos with every activity, stored at a reduced size
  • Your data is stored in Google Firebase in the EU, and you can delete all of it at any time

Questions and answers

What is Doelenboek?

Doelenboek is a free web app for parents who homeschool in Flanders, Belgium. You note down what you do with your children, link it to the Flemish minimum goals and see per child and per subject which goals have been covered and which are still open.

What are the minimum goals (minimumdoelen)?

The minimum goals are the learning goals the Flemish government sets for education: what children should at least know and be able to do. The official texts are published in Dutch on onderwijsdoelen.be. Doelenboek includes them by subject, with their code, so you can pick them for every activity.

Which minimum goals does Doelenboek include?

The minimum goals for preschool (kleuteronderwijs), for every subject: Dutch, mathematics, science & technology, history, geography, arts education, physical education and attitudes. The full lists are on this website, under Minimum goals.

Does Doelenboek cost anything?

No. Doelenboek is free and shows no ads. If you would like to support the project, you can make a voluntary donation on Ko-fi, which helps cover the hosting costs.

Where is my data stored?

Your children, activities and photos are stored in Google Firebase in the EU and can only be reached with your email address and password. The website does not store cookies on your device, and you can delete your account and all your data yourself at any time.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Doelenboek works in the browser on a computer, tablet or phone. Create an account with your email address and a password, and your data is on all your devices.

Is Doelenboek available in English?

Yes. You can switch between Dutch and English in the app. The minimum goals are shown in English as a translation of the official Dutch wording.

Does Doelenboek replace the official homeschooling rules?

No. Doelenboek helps you keep track of what your children learn, but it is not an official document. We do not take responsibility for the completeness of the minimum goals; the official lists are on onderwijsdoelen.be.
